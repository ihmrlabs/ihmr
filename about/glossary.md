# Glossary

The vocabulary we use, and where a term is still unsettled, we say so. A glossary that hides
open questions is doing you a disservice.

## What we are building

**IHMR** - India Health Maintenance Rail.

**Health maintenance** - continuous work to keep or improve someone's health over time, rather
than only treating episodes of illness when they arrive.

**Maintenance rail** - a shared infrastructure layer that many actors can participate in, owned
by no single provider, insurer, app, or department.

**Health state** - a structured picture of what is currently relevant about someone's health.
Guided by a principle we care about: *keep the minimum sufficient model of a person needed to
make good decisions* - not everything that could possibly be collected.

**Health objective** - what the system is trying to preserve or improve for someone. It changes
across a life; in palliative care it may shift entirely towards comfort, dignity, and avoiding
unnecessary intervention.

**Health obligation** - our working name for something the health system believes needs
attention. **Not** a legal obligation, a clinical mandate, or anything anyone is forced to do.
Who may create one, whether AI may propose one, when a clinician must approve it, and what
happens when someone declines are all genuinely open. This word is doing a lot of work and we
are watching it carefully.

**Intervention** - an action meant to resolve or advance an obligation. Sometimes the right
intervention is *nothing at all*.

**Fulfilment** - evidence that something actually happened. The loop does not close on "the
person was advised to do X."

**Resolution** - an obligation being addressed, deferred, declined, superseded, or otherwise
closed.

**Stakeholder inversion** - arranging funders and fulfillers around the person, instead of
asking the person to navigate every institution themselves.

**Fulfiller** - anyone who can carry out an intervention: a clinician, pharmacy, lab, community
health worker, hospital, digital service, or public programme.

**Funder** - anyone who can pay for one: government, an insurer, an employer, a programme, a
philanthropy, or the person themselves.

**Sovereign compute** - infrastructure whose critical operation stays under appropriate Indian
legal, technical, and operational control. Does not mean autarky.

**Healthy cities** - a research track asking whether a *place* can have a health state, its own
risks, and its own outstanding public-health obligations.

**Health baseline** - enough information about someone already alive for maintenance to begin.
The goal is a useful health state, not a database.

## How we work

**Status** - how sure we are about a document: `hypothesis`, `evidence`, `proposal`,
`specification`, `experiment`, `decision`, `superseded`.

**RFC** - Request for Comments. A proposal open for comment. Note that the IETF uses this term
for *finished* standards; we use the modern sense, meaning something still under discussion.

**Decision** - a position we have adopted for now, with its evidence, alternatives, and
reasoning recorded.

**Superseded** - replaced by something newer. Kept where you can find it, never deleted.

## India's health systems

**Aadhaar** - India's national identity system. We treat it as an identity root that makes a
person reliably addressable - explicitly **not** as a health database.

**ABDM** - Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission. India's digital health mission: health identity,
registries, consent, and health information exchange.

**ABHA** - Ayushman Bharat Health Account. Health identity within ABDM.

**HFR / HPR** - Health Facility Registry and Healthcare Professional Registry.

**UHI** - Unified Health Interface. Discovery and booking of health services.

**U-WIN** - India's digital immunisation platform. Interesting to us as an existing, narrow
example of obligation-like infrastructure: it can tell who is due, remind them, record what
happened, and follow up.

**PM-JAY** - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana. India's public health insurance scheme.

**AAM** - Ayushman Arogya Mandir. India's primary health and wellness centres.

**NHM** - National Health Mission.

**NP-NCD** - the national programme for prevention and control of non-communicable diseases.

**eSanjeevani** - India's national teleconsultation service.

**ASHA / ANM / AWW** - Accredited Social Health Activist, Auxiliary Nurse Midwife, and Anganwadi
Worker. Together, one of the largest community health workforces in the world.

**PHC / CHC** - Primary Health Centre, Community Health Centre.

**NCD** - non-communicable disease.

**DPI** - digital public infrastructure.

## Places we have learned from

**Healthier SG** - Singapore's national preventive health programme. A key reference for
longitudinal health planning and for the fiscal argument for prevention. Differs from our
thinking in being substantially doctor-anchored.

**Rwanda's community health model** - our main reference for last-mile delivery, and evidence
that community health workers plus coordination can work at national scale.

**Clalit** - Israeli health maintenance organisation, referenced for proactive population
health.
