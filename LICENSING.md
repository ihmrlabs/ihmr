# What is licensed, and how

Copyright (c) 2026 IHMR Labs and contributors.

Two licences, because this repository holds two different kinds of thing.

| What | Licence | File |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Research, documentation, specifications | CC BY 4.0 | [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) |
| Software | Apache 2.0 | [`LICENSE-CODE`](LICENSE-CODE) |

Under CC BY 4.0 you are free to share and adapt the research for any purpose, including
commercially, provided you give appropriate credit, link to the licence, and say if you changed
anything. Citing the DOI on a published version is the cleanest way to do that.

**Third-party material keeps its own licence.** Anything under `sources/` was published by
somebody else and is reproduced here under their terms, recorded in each file. Neither of the
licences above extends to it, and neither can.

**Data is not the same as our description of it.** Where we publish a dataset, our work on it -
the structure, the provenance record, the cleaning - is CC BY 4.0. The underlying figures belong
to whoever produced them, and their terms are recorded in that dataset's `SOURCE.md`.

If you are unsure whether something is yours to reuse, ask us at hello@projectihmr.org rather
than guess. We would far rather answer than have somebody either infringe or hold back
needlessly.
