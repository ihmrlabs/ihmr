# Essays

Our thinking. Long-form pieces written to be read start to finish, pulling together what we
have learned into an argument.

**An essay is thinking; research is work we have done.** We keep them apart on purpose, because
an essay may reach further than the evidence strictly supports - and you deserve to know which
you are reading.

Essays cite research notes rather than carrying their own evidence, so that correcting a note
does not mean rewriting an essay.

If you disagree with one, that is more or less what they are for.

Usually `hypothesis` or `proposal`.

## How essays are stored

Same structure as research: a folder per essay, a folder per published version, Markdown and a
generated PDF inside each. See [`research/README.md`](../research/README.md) for the reasoning.
