## A note on the date

The title says September 2026. Much of the data is not from 2026, and the article says so in its
first two paragraphs rather than burying it. India has not counted its population since 2011,
the most recent treatment-cascade measurements come from surveys fielded in 2017 to 2021, and
the newest household survey is still provisional.

That is a finding, not a caveat. A health system's ability to describe itself is part of its
condition.
