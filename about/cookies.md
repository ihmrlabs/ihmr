# Cookies

**Last updated: 3 September 2026**

Short version: we use one cookie to remember you have verified your email, and a small set of
analytics cookies **only if you say yes**. No advertising, no tracking you around the internet,
no third parties we have not named here.

If you say no, the site works exactly the same.

---

## What a cookie is

A small file a website asks your browser to keep, so it can recognise you next time. Some are
necessary for a site to function at all. Others watch what you do. Ours are described in full
below, with nothing left out.

## Cookies we set without asking

These are strictly necessary, which is why we do not ask. Without them the site cannot do the
job you came for.

| Cookie | What it does | How long |
|:--|:--|:--|
| `ihmr_session` | Remembers that you have verified your email address, so you do not have to enter a code every time you comment | 180 days |
| `ihmr_consent` | Remembers your answer to the analytics question, so we stop asking | 12 months |

`ihmr_session` contains a signed identifier and nothing else. No name, no email address, no
comment content. It cannot be read by any other site, and we do not use it to track you.

There is a small irony in setting a cookie to remember that you declined cookies. It is the only
way not to pester you.

## Cookies we set only if you agree

We use **PostHog** to understand how the site is actually used - which documents get read, where
people give up halfway, whether anyone finds the open questions. It genuinely helps us write
better.

**These do not load at all unless you agree.** Not blocked after loading, not loaded and
ignored. Simply not loaded.

| Cookie | What it does | How long |
|:--|:--|:--|
| `ph_*_posthog` | An anonymous identifier, so repeat visits are counted as one person rather than several | 12 months |

### What we have switched off

- **Session recording.** People write about health here, sometimes their own. Recording what
  someone types before they decide whether to post it would be indefensible, so it is off
  entirely
- **IP addresses** are anonymised before PostHog sees them
- **Autocapture of form contents.** We count that a form was used. We never capture what you
  typed into it

### Where it goes

PostHog's European region. Not India, because PostHog does not offer an Indian region.

Your identifying details - email address and phone number - are stored in India and never go to
PostHog. See [privacy](privacy.md).

## Things we do not have

- Advertising cookies
- Social media pixels from Meta, X, LinkedIn or anyone else
- Cross-site tracking
- Data brokers
- Anything that follows you off this site
- Embedded content that sets cookies without telling you

If we ever embed something from elsewhere that sets a cookie, this page will say so before it
happens.

## Changing your mind

Any time. There is a link in the site footer that reopens the choice, and turning analytics off
deletes the PostHog cookies immediately.

You can also clear cookies in your browser, or use Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control -
**we honour both**, and if either is set we will not even ask.

## Why we ask at all

India's DPDP Act treats analytics data as personal data requiring your consent, and the GDPR
requires it if you are in the UK or EU. So we would have to ask.

But we would ask anyway. It would be a strange project that argues carefully about consent in
health systems and then quietly dropped tracking cookies on people who came to read about it.
